Find SEO Issues, understand your site’s structure and turn crawl data into a practical plan.

Tadpole is an open-source desktop SEO crawler for SEO specialists, website owners and developers. Inspect pages, follow internal links and triage grouped Issues in a native Linux workspace. Your crawl results stay on your machine, ready to search, filter, save and export.

**Triage Issues by Severity**

* Review crawl and sitemap findings together in the dedicated Issues workspace, grouped into named Issues across four fixed Severity tiers (Critical, High, Medium, Low).
* Inspect transparent Severity counts, Affected URL percentages, Rationale, Suggested Actions and per-URL Evidence—without an arbitrary 0–100 site health score.
* Jump directly from any Affected URL into Crawl Results or Sitemap & Discovery.

**Audit the pages that matter**

* Crawl a website, a path, one page or your own list of URLs.
* Inspect HTTP status, indexability, titles, descriptions, canonicals, headings and images.
* Review Open Graph and Twitter metadata, JSON-LD diagnostics and extracted Markdown.
* Compare XML and gzip sitemaps with internal-link reachability, review candidate orphan pages and export discovery Evidence.
* Set URL and depth limits, concurrency, include/exclude patterns and robots.txt handling.

**Understand how your site connects**

* Explore directory treemaps and icicles, link-depth trees, URL link neighborhoods, section-link matrices and depth-versus-internal-rank plots.
* Investigate inlinks and outlinks and find potential internal-link opportunities.
* Move from a site overview to the pages behind it.

**Take your findings with you**

* Export developer-ready Issues reports to Markdown (or copy straight to the clipboard) and uncapped CSV for the full audit or a single Issue.
* Search and filter Crawl Results, export the filtered table to CSV or copy it to a spreadsheet, and export page content as Markdown.
* Save and reopen `.tadpole` Projects (with legacy `.seocrawl` Projects still readable) via the file dialog, double-click or CLI.

**Optional AI suggestions, under your control**

Request page-level title, description and text-based image alt suggestions through OpenRouter or OpenAI. Ordinary crawling needs no AI account. AI actions send selected page content to your chosen provider, require your own API key and are billed by that provider. Credentials stay in memory for the session and are excluded from saved Projects.

Tadpole is MIT-licensed. Its direction comes from an experienced SEO professional, with development carried out primarily through AI coding agents and reviewed after updates.

Severity tiers and internal rank are local audit guidance, not Google scores. Schema checks do not certify rich-result eligibility. JavaScript rendering requires an accessible Chrome/Chromium executable, which is not bundled; availability inside Snap confinement needs separate verification.
