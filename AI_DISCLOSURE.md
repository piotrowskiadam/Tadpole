# AI development disclosure

Tadpole has been developed primarily by orchestrating AI coding agents, including Google's Antigravity, OpenAI's Codex, and OpenCode. Significant parts of the application source code, tests, documentation, packaging and visual design have been created or changed with AI assistance. The contribution is substantial across the project; the repository history records individual changes, but we do not claim a precise percentage of AI-generated lines.

The project's ideas and feature direction come from an SEO professional with many years of experience in the field. The project owner directs the agents and checks the application after each update. Automated tests and human checks support this process; they should not be interpreted as a guarantee that every line has been reviewed individually or that the application has no defects.

This disclosure concerns **how Tadpole is developed**. The application's optional AI metadata suggestions are a separate feature: they require a user-supplied API key and are initiated by the user. See the README's privacy and cost information for what is sent to the selected provider.

This disclosure document was itself prepared with AI assistance, based on information provided by the project owner. Where a distribution platform requires a specific disclosure or submission process, the submitter must follow that platform's current rules and provide their own accurate account of the affected material.
