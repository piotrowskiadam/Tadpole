# Tadpole

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Native desktop application using GTK4 and Libadwaita, packaged for Linux and Windows. This is not a mobile or browser application.

## Users and purpose

Tadpole is an SEO crawler and site-auditing tool. Its owner is an experienced SEO professional who defines features and checks updates. Users inspect crawl results, metadata, indexability and internal linking, then export findings or explore site structure.

## Capabilities and constraints

- Crawl websites and inspect pages in a dense results table, filters, detail panels and summary dashboard.
- Explore directory structure, internal linking and rank with the site's visualization workspace.
- Optional page-level AI suggestions use explicitly initiated provider requests and owner-supplied credentials.
- SEO Studio was removed at the owner's request. Future DataForSEO features require a specific brief.
- Linux distribution includes upstream Flatpak bundles; a Flathub submission remains a separate human-owned process subject to Flathub's AI policy.
- Media mode is explicitly enabled for demo screenshots and short recordings. It starts with labeled fictional data and a separate application ID. Desktop/audio recording and high-frame-rate video are outside its current scope.

## Brand commitments

Preserve the Tadpole name, established green mascot and GTK/Libadwaita interface. The owner chose logo-led and app-view store banners with “Explore your site. Find what matters.” Native tool copy remains English.

## Evidence and open decisions

Product evidence comes from the owner's requests, README, DESIGN.md and implemented modules. The banner requirements were supplied in the owner's Snapcraft screenshot. Performance claims, customer proof, pricing and new DataForSEO capabilities have not been specified; do not invent them.
